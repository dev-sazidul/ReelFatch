# Backlink opportunities

Date checked: 12 November 2026

Every item below is a **prospect**. None is a verified backlink. Status in the tracker is `Prospecting` until a person submits, an editor approves, and the live page is opened.

Domain authority and similar scores are blank on purpose. Roundup articles published their own DR or traffic figures. Those numbers were not remeasured and are not repeated here.

## How a prospect qualifies

Keep a site when most of these are true:

- The page is about software, web apps, creator tools, digital literacy, or copyright.
- A named editor, a submission form, or a community rule explains how something gets published.
- The site has a real about page and a history of articles that are not only paid posts.
- The audience could use a public-link downloader without being told to break a platform's rules.
- The link would be in the body of a relevant page, or in a directory profile the site itself maintains.

Drop a site when any of these are true:

- The offer is "publish in 24 hours on tier-1 news sites."
- The page requires a badge or a link back before it will mention ReelFetch. That is a link exchange. A 2026 Launcholio directory table says some launch sites ask for this. Re-read the destination's current rules. Do not add a sitewide badge to bestdownloader.site just to be listed.
- The site sells dofollow packages, accepts any topic, or hides the author.
- The only contact is a bulk guest-post form covering health, finance, casino, and tech together.
- The domain is a lookalike of a known downloader brand.

A university "write for us" page that was found in passing, Greater Good at UC Berkeley, says it rarely accepts unsolicited pitches and does not want articles that promote an app. That is a useful negative example: a famous domain is not an opportunity.

## 2. Launch and maker communities

These can create a brand mention. They often use nofollow or do not link out at all. That is still useful when the conversation is real. It is not a reason to post the same announcement everywhere in one day.

| Place | URL | Relevant use | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Indie Hackers | https://www.indiehackers.com/ | A build log about an honest downloader: what the tool will not fetch, how takedowns work | Follow their self-promotion norms. No traffic claims. |
| Hacker News, Show HN | https://news.ycombinator.com/ | Only if there is a technical story, for example how public links are checked and why private media is refused | One post. Stay for the comments. Do not ask friends to vote. |
| Reddit r/SideProject | https://www.reddit.com/r/SideProject/ | A short build note after reading the current sidebar rules | Many subreddits remove bare promotion. Reddit's vote manipulation rules apply. |

Do not post the same launch text on r/YouTube, r/Instagram, or r/TikTok. Those communities often ban downloader promotion, and the post would fight the platforms' own rules.

## 3. Technology blogs and guest posts

No publication is listed here as "accepts guest posts" because a write-for-us page for this niche was not verified on 12 November 2026.

The search that looked for contributor guidelines did not produce a relevant, reputable technology blog with an open guest-post policy. It did surface a page offering paid placement on "tier-1" sites within 24 hours. That offer is out of scope. Do not buy it.

How to find a real one later:

1. Search for the topic plus `"write for us"` or `"contributor guidelines"`, then open the guidelines page.
2. Read ten recent articles. Reject the site if they are interchangeable, stuffed with exact-match anchors, or signed by names that do not exist elsewhere.
3. Record the guidelines URL in the tracker before drafting a custom intro.
4. Pitch one of the topics in `GUEST_POST_STRATEGY.md`. Attach a draft only after the editor asks for it.

Likely audience categories, not named outlets:

- Digital-literacy blogs for teachers and librarians
- Creator-economy newsletters that explain platform rules
- Independent web-performance or browser blogs, for the "what a download actually is" draft
- University IT or library blogs that already publish student how-tos and that allow an external citation

## 4. Resource pages

Do not collect "best free tools" pages from a scraper. A resource page is worth a personal email only when all of this is true:

- The page is maintained, with dates or a recent addition.
- ReelFetch would sit beside similar websites, not beside unrelated SEO tools.
- The page already links out because the links are the content.
- You can name the specific section the link would improve.

Suggested search, to be run by a person and logged:

- `"public domain" video classroom tools`
- `"save your own" reel OR short teacher`
- `"online tools" "media literacy" inurl:resources`

Put each accepted page in the tracker as `Prospecting` with the source URL. If the page's existing downloader link is dead, use the broken-link template only after you confirm the old URL fails.

## 5. Business and startup directories

General startup directories are a low priority. ReelFetch is a consumer web tool, not a funded company profile. Submit only where the form can be completed truthfully:

- No employee count, funding round, or office address unless those facts exist.
- No logo that implies a relationship with YouTube, Meta, TikTok, or the other platforms.
- Category: web application or consumer utility, not "enterprise video platform."

BetaList and similar launch directories were named in public roundups. Their current fee and editorial rules were not re-read. Check the fee before spending money. A paid review is not a paid link. If the only product is a dofollow placement, decline.

## 6. Forums and communities

Useful places are those where a maintainer can answer "can I keep a copy of a video I posted?" The answer can mention ReelFetch once.

Avoid:

- Signature links in old forums
- Quora or Reddit answers that exist only to carry the URL
- Facebook groups that require a link drop to join
- Any thread asking how to download private or paid videos. The correct reply is that ReelFetch will not do that, with no promotional link.

## 7. Digital PR that fits the brand

Possible stories, if and when they are true:

- A clear written policy for takedown requests, already at `/copyright`
- A short note on what the PWA install does and does not store
- A correction when a roundup lists ReelFetch with a feature it does not have

Do not invent a usage statistic, a user count, or a "most popular downloader" headline.

## 8. Rejected patterns

| Pattern | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Paid guest posts on news domains with a 24-hour turnaround | Reject |
| Badge or reciprocal link required before a listing | Reject unless an editor independently wants the link and the badge is not sitewide |
| Comment spam on competitor blogs | Reject |
| Mass submission to hundreds of directories | Reject |
| Fake reviews on G2, Trustpilot, or app stores | Reject. ReelFetch also has no native store app to review |
| Links from downloader mirror networks | Reject |
