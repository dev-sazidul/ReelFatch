## What to post

One useful post a week is enough. Repeat the limits more often than the invitation to try the site.

| Network | A useful post | Do not post |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Facebook | A single platform tip, such as "a Facebook share link still has to be public" with the Facebook guide | Viral "download anything" claims, other people's clips |
| LinkedIn | A short note for creators or educators about permission versus a public URL | Funding, user-count, or growth claims that were not measured |
| Pinterest | A static card that links to one platform guide and states the limit in the image text | Pins of copyrighted videos |
| X | A correction or a one-sentence limit, linking to the FAQ or copyright page | Trending-audio bait, reply spam under celebrity posts |
| YouTube | A screen recording of the operator's own public video, including a failure case | Compilations of other creators, "no copyright" music over their footage |

Put the destination in the first comment or the normal link field. Do not hide it in a shortener.

## Communities

Participate where the topic is already "how do I keep a copy of something I made?" Introduce the site when it answers that question.

| Place | How to show up | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Indie Hackers | A build log: decisions about private media, takedowns, and honest quality language | Revenue screenshots you do not have |
| Hacker News | Show HN only for a concrete technical write-up, then answer comments | Asking for upvotes |
| Reddit | Read the sidebar. Prefer a relevant build or help thread over a launch blast | r/YouTube, r/Instagram, and similar spaces that prohibit this promotion |
| Platform developer forums | Only to discuss public, documented behavior. Do not imply a partnership | Posting tokens, cookies, or sample URLs of someone else's private media |

If a community forbids the link, answer the question without it.
